# ADR 039 - Managed video is reusable media published through content

- **Status**: accepted
- **Date**: 2026-11-23
- **Builds on**: [ADR 002](002-single-postgres-rls-tenancy.md) (site isolation and portability), [ADR 018](018-precise-editorial-operations.md) (publication, access and caching), [ADR 022](022-managed-extensions-and-site-integrations.md) (managed integrations) and [ADR 024](024-managed-integrations-and-external-ownership.md) (provider ownership and reconciliation)

## Context

A managed embed can play a Bunny recording in Mycel today. The same recording needs to be reusable in several documents without duplicating credits, poster or captions. Direct upload and streaming introduce storage and delivery outside Mycel's blob store. The video needs an explicit owner and access boundary so its representations follow the same rules as the item that publishes it.

## Decision

1. **Mycel owns the video record and the editorial data.** The site's media library holds a stable video id, provider binding, processing status, duration, localised title, chosen poster, recording credit and licence, and optional external watch links. A new `video` block refers to the video id in the same site and carries its own localised caption. An ordinary article or page owns the story, its URL and its per-locale publication. Creating or encoding a video grants no public URL. Existing `video-embed` blocks keep their schema, consent and rendering, including Bunny iframe embeds.
2. **Bunny Stream retains the original and owns encoding and delivery behind an adapter.** The platform operates one account with a dedicated library per site and EU storage and processing. The server resolves the library and credentials from the authorized site, including when it checks readiness or renews an upload. Library credentials stay server-side. A resumable upload goes directly from the client to Bunny using an expiring grant for one video in that library. A request body, provider id or client-reported completion cannot establish ownership or readiness. Per-site storage and stored minutes are entitlements under [ADR 015](015-entitlements-as-data.md). Before granting upload, account for pending reservations and check the applicable limits. Reconcile with provider-reported duration and stored bytes, including the retained original and MP4 fallback, and withhold availability if the reconciled total exceeds either entitlement. Keep ambiguous provider writes visible and reconcile them before retry under [ADR 019](019-supporter-consistency-identity-and-retention.md). See [Bunny's resumable upload contract](https://docs.bunny.net/stream/tus-resumable-uploads).
3. **The native video element owns the controls.** Prefer native HLS where supported. Enhance with a site-served HLS client on browsers that support Media Source Extensions when native HLS is unavailable. Keep a real encoded MP4 for unsupported or failed HLS and for playback without JavaScript. A video is ready for this contract when the required outputs exist. Use the site's CORS and CSP integration checks for the configured delivery origin and all resources HLS needs. The initial page shows a same-site poster, reserves the player space and starts no provider request before the visitor chooses to play and any applicable site consent permits it. An ordinary external watch link, when supplied, is an explicit navigation. See [Bunny's MP4 fallback](https://docs.bunny.net/stream/mp4-downloads) and [HLS client compatibility and CORS](https://github.com/video-dev/hls.js#compatibility).
4. **Access is checked where playback is granted.** Resolve the reference through the published document in the requested locale and through its access decision. Initially play only from eligible public documents for which the site's integration policy permits delivery. Keep draft, preview and restricted contexts free of provider requests and delivery grants. Protect direct provider delivery, including HLS playlists and segments, MP4, and any provider image used by the adapter. Keep short-lived delivery grants out of shared caches, feeds, search, previews and outbound mail. A placement removed from public view cannot mint more grants. Previously issued grants can survive until expiry. Changes to the live video projection must invalidate affected public representations. For unavailable playback, keep the caption and any published transcript or approved alternative link readable. See [Bunny's direct-delivery security](https://docs.bunny.net/stream/security).
5. **Mycel publishes transcripts per locale.** A transcript is structured timed text with speaker and sound information as applicable. Editors import timed text or author it in Mycel. AI assistance may produce a draft through Mycel's approved EU path under [ADR 021](021-assistance-is-a-suggestion.md). Provider transcription is outside this workflow. An authorized editor explicitly publishes a transcript for a language. From that published source Mycel serves versioned WebVTT tracks for native captions or subtitles and can render a readable transcript alongside editorial context. Draft cues stay out of track URLs, search and public documents. Tracks and readable text follow the same access rules as playback. Time links, chapters and follow-along reading reuse the cue times.
6. **The editor selects a preferred watch page per video and locale.** Findability work will emit `VideoObject` and video sitemap entries only for that explicitly selected, public and indexable article or page with the video as primary content in that locale. Resolve the reference at read time. An ineligible or missing selection emits neither representation. Other placements keep their own canonical URLs. Use the selected watch URL for a video permalink or newsletter thumbnail when available, with the placing article's eligible URL or an approved external watch link as fallbacks. Search delivery will need an explicit policy for stable crawlable assets before activation. See [Google's watch-page criteria](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/video#watch-page).
7. **Portability and removal cover both systems.** Tenant export includes video records, placement references, transcripts, poster assets and the retained source bytes in the complete tenant archive. A missing required source fails the export visibly. Keep credentials and signed URLs out of the archive. Import preserves Mycel ids and restores playback only after the new installation has authorized and verified a binding in the importing site's library, or re-uploaded the exported source there. A video in use cannot be trashed. Trashing an unused record cannot delete remote bytes. Provider deletion is a separate authorized and auditable effect with reconciliation. An erasure that requires removing a provider copy remains pending until that removal is confirmed.

## Rejected alternatives

- **A dedicated video content type** would give the same video another publication surface even when its story already has an article or page.
- **Bunny's iframe as the managed player's contract** leaves playback integration and cue synchronization outside Mycel. It remains suitable for the existing embed.
- **A shared Bunny library or public provider addresses copied into blocks** cannot represent site ownership, independent quotas or the publication check.
- **Separate editable captions at the provider** would let two versions of the same spoken text diverge.

## Consequences

- The first viewing trial uses the same recording in an ordinary article and an ordinary page. Prove native Safari HLS, adaptive playback in Chromium under bandwidth throttling, a Mycel WebVTT track inline and in fullscreen, and the MP4 in a browser context with JavaScript disabled.
- A published poster may come from a provider-selected frame or the site's image library. A styled share poster, transcript authoring, chapters, timed navigation and video indexing can build on the same media reference. Member-only video requires its own reviewed delivery policy.
